About me

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Tsinghua University. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2010 and joined Tsinghua in 2017.

My research focuses on statistical learning theory and the mathematical foundations of deep learning. Recent work studies kernel methods, neural tangent kernels, adaptive feature learning, and the generalization of overparameterized models. I also work on high-dimensional inference and sufficient dimension reduction, particularly sliced inverse regression.

See my published work and preprints, explore my research projects, or visit the teaching and service page. For research discussions, please contact me by email.
