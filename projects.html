Research projects

Related research

Recent papers develop these interests in several directions:

  • Kernel methods and generalization: optimal rates of kernel ridge regression and saturation effects of spectral algorithms.
  • Adaptive feature learning: adaptivity through overparameterization and spectral algorithms with learned kernels.
  • Sufficient dimension reduction: sparse sliced inverse regression via lasso and the structural dimension of sliced inverse regression.
